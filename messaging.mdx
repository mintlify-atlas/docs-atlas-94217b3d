---
title: "Messaging"
description: "Send and receive messages, react, reply, and manage your conversations in Delta Chat."
---

Delta Chat conversations work like any modern chat app — but every message is routed over email and encrypted end-to-end automatically.

## Sending messages

Tap the compose button (pencil icon) on the chat list to start a new conversation, or open an existing chat and type in the message field at the bottom.

- **Text** — Type and tap send. Messages are delivered as encrypted email.
- **Emoji** — Open the emoji picker from the keyboard or long-press the send button.
- **Voice message** — Hold the microphone icon to record; release to send. Tap to cancel.
- **Stickers** — Tap the sticker icon in the attachment bar to open the sticker picker.
- **Attachments** — Tap the paperclip icon to attach images, videos, files, or a contact card.

## Replying and quoting

Swipe right on any message to quote it in your reply. The quoted text appears above your new message so recipients see the context.

You can also long-press a message and tap **Reply** from the context menu.

## Reactions

Long-press a message and select an emoji to send a reaction. Reactions are visible to all participants and trigger a `DC_EVENT_REACTIONS_CHANGED` event in the app.

## Forwarding messages

Long-press a message, tap **Forward**, then choose the destination chat. You can forward to multiple chats at once using the multi-select checkboxes.

## Message status

Each outgoing message shows a status indicator:

| Icon | State | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Clock | Pending (`DC_STATE_OUT_PENDING`) | Queued, not yet sent |
| Single check | Delivered (`DC_STATE_OUT_DELIVERED`) | Received by the server |
| Double check | Read (`DC_STATE_OUT_MDN_RCVD`) | Opened by the recipient |
| Warning | Failed (`DC_STATE_OUT_FAILED`) | Delivery error — tap to retry |

<Note>
  Read receipts require the recipient to also use Delta Chat. If they read your message in a standard email client, only the single-check "delivered" state is shown.
</Note>

## Deleting messages

Long-press a message and tap **Delete**. You can:
- **Delete for me** — removes the message from your device only.
- **Delete for everyone** — sends a delete request to all participants (only works if they use Delta Chat).

A `DC_EVENT_MSG_DELETED` event causes the notification to be removed automatically when a sender deletes a message.

## Disappearing (ephemeral) messages

Set a timer so messages self-delete after they've been read:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open chat settings">
    In a chat, tap the chat name at the top to open the chat profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the timer">
    Tap **Disappearing messages** and choose a duration (1 minute, 1 hour, 1 day, 1 week, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Messages auto-delete">
    New messages in this chat are deleted from all devices after the timer expires, starting from when the message was read.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Both sides must use Delta Chat for the timer to work reliably. The timer is shown as a system message in the chat when changed.
</Tip>

## Muting chats

To silence notifications from a chat without leaving it, long-press the chat in the chat list and tap **Mute**. Choose a duration or mute indefinitely.

Muted chats still receive messages — they just don't produce sound or vibration.

## Pinning and archiving

- **Pin** — Long-press a chat and tap **Pin** to keep it at the top of your list. Up to a handful of chats can be pinned.
- **Archive** — Swipe a chat left and tap **Archive** to move it out of the main list. Archived chats appear under the "Archived chats" entry at the bottom of the list.

## Searching

Tap the search icon at the top of the chat list to search across all your conversations. You can also search within a single chat by tapping the magnifier in the chat toolbar.

Search is case-insensitive and works with non-ASCII characters.
