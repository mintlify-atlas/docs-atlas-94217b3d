---
title: "Multiple Profiles"
description: "Run multiple independent chat identities in a single Delta Chat app."
---

Delta Chat lets you maintain multiple completely separate profiles — each with its own email account, contacts, chats, and encryption keys — all within a single app.

## What is a profile?

A profile is a self-contained Delta Chat identity tied to one email address. Profiles are fully isolated: messages, contacts, and keys from one profile are never mixed with another.

Common use cases:
- Separate **work** and **personal** identities
- A **private** identity using an anonymous Chatmail address
- **Testing** with a throwaway account
- Keeping a **family** account distinct from a professional one

## Adding a new profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the profile switcher">
    Tap your avatar or profile icon in the top-left corner of the chat list to open the profile switcher.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add profile">
    Tap **Add Profile** (the "+" button) at the bottom of the switcher.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the new profile">
    Follow the standard [profile setup flow](/setup-profile) — either instant onboarding or connect an existing email account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching between profiles

Tap your avatar in the top-left to open the profile switcher, then tap any profile to switch to it. The app instantly loads the selected profile's chat list.

<Tip>
  You can switch profiles even while sharing or forwarding a message — Delta Chat lets you redirect to any profile mid-action.
</Tip>

## Notifications from multiple profiles

Delta Chat delivers notifications for all profiles simultaneously. Each notification is clearly labelled with the profile name so you always know which account received the message.

## Deleting a profile

Deleting a profile permanently removes all its messages, contacts, and keys from your device.

<Warning>
  Profile deletion is protected by your device's system lock or PIN to prevent accidental data loss. Make sure you have a [backup](/backup-and-restore) before deleting.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open profile settings">
    Switch to the profile you want to delete, then go to **Settings → Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete account">
    Tap **Delete Account** and confirm with your device PIN or biometrics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Profile isolation

Each profile has its own:
- Email account and credentials
- Encryption keys (OpenPGP)
- Contact list and chat history
- Notification settings
- Webxdc apps and their state

Profiles do **not** share anything with each other by design.
