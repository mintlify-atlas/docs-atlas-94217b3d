## Why Delta Chat?

Delta Chat solves the problem of messenger fragmentation and centralized control. Because it runs over email, you can chat with anyone who has an email address — even if they don't have Delta Chat installed — while getting a modern, private messenger experience.

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Download Delta Chat from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=chat.delta) or [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/app/com.b44t.messenger). See [Installation](/installation) for all options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your profile">
    Enter your email address and password to connect Delta Chat to your email account. See [Set Up Your Profile](/setup-profile) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Tap the compose button, search for a contact by email, and send your first encrypted message. See [Messaging](/messaging) to learn more.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite friends">
    Share your QR code or invite link so contacts can verify your identity and join encrypted chats. See [Contact Verification](/contact-verification).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Delta Chat works with any existing email account — Gmail, Outlook, Fastmail, self-hosted, or a dedicated [Chatmail](https://chatmail.at) server optimized for Delta Chat.
</Note>
