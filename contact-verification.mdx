Delta Chat lets you establish **verified contacts** — contacts whose identity you have confirmed in person or through a trusted channel. Verified contacts give you a stronger security guarantee than ordinary encrypted chats: you know that you are talking to the right person, not an impostor who happened to get hold of your contact's email address.

## What verification means

When you verify a contact, Delta Chat performs a cryptographic handshake (the **SecureJoin** protocol) that ties that contact's identity to a specific public key. After verification:

- A **green checkmark** appears next to the verified contact's name.
- Messages in your chat with that contact are end-to-end encrypted and the key is confirmed — not just trusted on first use.
- If a key mismatch is detected later (for example, after a device change), Delta Chat warns you immediately.

<Info>
  Verification is optional. Regular Delta Chat chats are already end-to-end encrypted automatically. Verification adds the additional assurance that the key belongs to the person you physically met or exchanged QR codes with.
</Info>

## Verifying a contact via QR code

The most common way to verify a contact is to scan each other's QR codes in person, or to share your QR code image through a trusted side channel.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show your QR code">
    Open Delta Chat and tap the QR code icon in the chat list header, or go to **Settings → QR Code**. Your personal invite QR code is displayed on screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan your contact's QR code">
    Ask your contact to display their QR code. In Delta Chat, tap the QR scan icon and point the camera at their screen.

    Alternatively, if you have a QR code image saved from your contact, tap **Load from image** to scan it without a camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the dialog">
    Delta Chat shows a dialog asking you to confirm that you want to start a verified chat with that person (`DC_QR_ASK_VERIFYCONTACT = 200`). Tap **OK** to proceed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the SecureJoin handshake">
    Delta Chat exchanges a series of signed messages in the background to confirm the keys on both sides. This takes a few seconds. Once complete, both of you see a confirmation and the contact is marked as verified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Both you and your contact need to be online during the SecureJoin handshake. Keep Delta Chat open and connected until the process finishes.
</Note>

## Joining a verified group via QR code

A verified group is a group chat where all members have been verified against the group creator's key. To join one:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Obtain the group invite QR code">
    Ask the group admin to show or send you the group's invite QR code. The admin generates this from inside the group chat: tap the group name at the top, then **Invite via QR Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    Open Delta Chat, tap the QR scan icon, and scan the group invite code. Delta Chat recognizes this as a group invite (`DC_QR_ASK_VERIFYGROUP = 202`) and shows you the group name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and join">
    Tap **OK** in the dialog. The SecureJoin handshake runs between you and the group admin, and you are added to the group once the verification completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only the group admin can generate the invite QR code for a verified group. If you receive a group invite link from an untrusted source, inspect it carefully before joining.
</Warning>

## Fingerprint verification

Each Delta Chat account has a unique cryptographic fingerprint — a long hexadecimal string that uniquely identifies the public key for that account. You can use fingerprints to verify a contact manually, without a QR code.

To view a contact's fingerprint, open a chat with them, tap the contact's name at the top, and scroll to the **Encryption Info** section.

When you scan a QR code that contains only a fingerprint (without an email address), Delta Chat shows the fingerprint for you to compare manually (`DC_QR_FPR_WITHOUT_ADDR`).

### Fingerprint scan results

| Result | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `DC_QR_FPR_OK` (210) | The scanned fingerprint matches the key you already have for this contact. The contact's key is confirmed. |
| `DC_QR_FPR_MISMATCH` (220) | The scanned fingerprint does **not** match the key on file. This could indicate a key change or, in the worst case, an impersonation attempt. Do not proceed without clarifying this with your contact through a different channel. |

<Warning>
  A fingerprint mismatch (`DC_QR_FPR_MISMATCH`) is a serious security signal. Stop the conversation and reach out to your contact by phone or another trusted method to confirm what happened before continuing.
</Warning>

## How SecureJoin works behind the scenes

SecureJoin is the cryptographic protocol Delta Chat uses to establish verified contacts and groups. When you scan a contact's QR code and confirm, the following happens:

1. Your device sends a signed join request to the contact.
2. The contact's device validates your key and replies with a signed acknowledgment.
3. Your device validates the acknowledgment against the fingerprint from the QR code.
4. Both devices confirm the verification is complete and record the verified status.

All messages in this handshake are end-to-end encrypted and signed, so a compromised server cannot interfere with or forge the verification.

<Info>
  SecureJoin is based on the countermeasures against impersonation attacks described in research on secure group communication. Delta Chat's implementation is auditable in the open-source core library.
</Info>

## Withdrawing and revoking invite links

If you have shared a QR invite code and want to stop new contacts from using it — for example, after posting it publicly by mistake — you can withdraw it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Show the QR code screen">
    Go to **Settings → QR Code** or open the group and tap **Invite via QR Code**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Withdraw">
    Tap the **Withdraw** option (shown in red). This invalidates the current invite link (`DC_QR_WITHDRAW_VERIFYCONTACT = 500` for contacts, `DC_QR_WITHDRAW_VERIFYGROUP = 502` for groups).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a new code if needed">
    After withdrawing, you can generate a fresh QR code that replaces the old one. Anyone who previously saved the old code can no longer use it to join or verify.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also revive (re-enable) a withdrawn invite link if you decide you want to use it again, using the revive option on the same screen.
</Tip>

## Blocked contacts

If someone contacts you without your consent or sends unwanted messages, you can block them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact's profile">
    Tap the contact's name at the top of the chat, or find them in **Settings → Blocked Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Block the contact">
    Tap **Block Contact**. Blocked contacts cannot send you messages and do not appear in your contact list. Their messages are silently discarded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unblock if needed">
    To unblock someone, go to **Settings → Blocked Contacts**, find the contact, and tap **Unblock**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Blocking a contact does not delete your existing chat history. If you unblock the contact later, the chat history is still accessible.
</Note>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to verify contacts to use Delta Chat?">
    No. Delta Chat encrypts all messages automatically using Autocrypt without any verification step. Verification is an optional additional layer for users who need stronger identity assurance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if a verified contact gets a new device?">
    When a contact sets up Delta Chat on a new device, they get a new key pair. The next time you send them a message, Delta Chat may show a warning that their key has changed. To re-establish trust, verify the contact again by scanning each other's QR codes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I verify a contact remotely without meeting in person?">
    You can share your QR code image through a channel you trust — for example, a video call where you display the code on screen. This is less secure than an in-person scan but stronger than trusting a key with no verification at all.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a verified contact and a verified group?">
    A verified contact means you have confirmed that specific person's key. A verified group additionally ensures that every member of the group has been verified by the group admin, and that the group membership itself is protected from tampering.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
